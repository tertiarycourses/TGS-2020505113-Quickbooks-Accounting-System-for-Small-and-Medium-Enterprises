# Activity 09 checklist

- [ ] Step 1: Confirm the reporting period, basis, comparison period, class/location filters and rounding convention.
- [ ] Step 2: Run the Profit and Loss report and compare revenue, gross margin and operating expenses with the workbook.
- [ ] Step 3: Run the Balance Sheet as at month-end and verify that Assets equal Liabilities plus Equity.
- [ ] Step 4: Reconcile cash, AR, AP, inventory and GST control accounts to their supporting schedules.
- [ ] Step 5: Run AR and AP Ageing Summary using the same as-of date.
- [ ] Step 6: Trace one material balance from each report to underlying transactions.
- [ ] Step 7: Customise and save a management report with relevant columns and comparison data.
- [ ] Step 8: Identify three business insights and distinguish an observation from a supported conclusion.
- [ ] Step 9: Assign an owner, action and due date to each material issue.
- [ ] Step 10: Complete the Report Validation checklist and reviewer sign-off.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Report parameters are consistent
- [ ] Balance Sheet equation holds
- [ ] Control accounts reconcile
- [ ] Insights link to evidence and actions
