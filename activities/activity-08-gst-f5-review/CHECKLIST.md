# Activity 08 checklist

- [ ] Step 1: Confirm the quarter, accounting basis and period lock in the GST Review workbook.
- [ ] Step 2: Run GST summary, GST detail and transactions-without-tax reports for the same period.
- [ ] Step 3: Reconcile taxable sales and purchases to the relevant P&L/ledger totals.
- [ ] Step 4: Reconcile output GST and input GST to the GST control accounts.
- [ ] Step 5: Investigate differences caused by dates, rounding, credits, journals or changes to prior periods.
- [ ] Step 6: Populate the F5 Control sheet from validated QuickBooks report totals; do not manually override formulas.
- [ ] Step 7: Trace at least three sales and three purchase items from report to source document.
- [ ] Step 8: Review the exception log and confirm that every material item is corrected or escalated.
- [ ] Step 9: Record the preparer and independent reviewer sign-off.
- [ ] Step 10: Retain the review pack; filing on myTax Portal is outside this training activity.

## Acceptance
- [ ] GST detail agrees to control accounts
- [ ] F5 control figures are formula-driven
- [ ] Sample evidence is traceable
- [ ] Preparer and reviewer sign-offs are complete
