# Activity 05 checklist

- [ ] Step 1: Review the Purchase Register and identify the approved supplier, item, quantity, price, GST and approver.
- [ ] Step 2: Create purchase order PO-2001 and confirm that it is non-posting until receipt/bill conversion.
- [ ] Step 3: Compare the purchase order, receiving evidence and supplier invoice using the three-way match sheet.
- [ ] Step 4: Convert the purchase order to bill BILL-2001; correct any variance only with an approved reason.
- [ ] Step 5: Enter the immediate-payment office expense separately and use the correct expense account and GST code.
- [ ] Step 6: Pay the approved bill from the DBS operating account and record the supplier reference.
- [ ] Step 7: Enter the supplier credit for damaged goods and apply it to the supplier balance.
- [ ] Step 8: Create a reminder-type recurring transaction for the monthly internet bill with a defined end/review date.
- [ ] Step 9: Run AP Ageing Summary and compare supplier balances with the workbook control totals.
- [ ] Step 10: Record evidence and exceptions, including any plan-specific workflow difference.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Three-way match is documented
- [ ] Bill and payment retain source references
- [ ] Supplier credit is applied
- [ ] Recurring template has an owner and review date
