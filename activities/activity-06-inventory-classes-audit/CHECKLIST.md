# Activity 06 checklist

- [ ] Step 1: Review the Inventory Roll-forward sheet and confirm opening quantity and cost for each item.
- [ ] Step 2: Compare purchases, sales, returns and adjustments with the expected closing quantities.
- [ ] Step 3: Investigate negative stock, unusual adjustment dates and unsupported changes; do not post a plug adjustment.
- [ ] Step 4: Apply the approved class and location taxonomy to the sample transactions where the training plan supports it.
- [ ] Step 5: Run a transaction report filtered for blank class/location and record the exceptions.
- [ ] Step 6: Open the audit log and filter for the activity period and transaction changes.
- [ ] Step 7: Trace one created, one edited and one voided/deleted example to its user and timestamp.
- [ ] Step 8: Assess whether the user role and activity are appropriate under the access matrix.
- [ ] Step 9: Document corrective actions and training needs in the Audit Review sheet.
- [ ] Step 10: Sign off the inventory and audit controls only when supporting evidence is complete.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Inventory roll-forward reconciles
- [ ] Unclassified items are resolved or logged
- [ ] Audit samples identify user and change
- [ ] Corrective actions have owner and due date
