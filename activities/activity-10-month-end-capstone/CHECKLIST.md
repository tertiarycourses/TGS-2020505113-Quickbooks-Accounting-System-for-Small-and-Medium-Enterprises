# Activity 10 checklist

- [ ] Step 1: Accept the capstone handover and verify the source-file inventory against the Intake Register.
- [ ] Step 2: Import or review outstanding master-data and bank items, resolving only approved exceptions.
- [ ] Step 3: Complete the sales and purchase cut-off checks and identify missing or duplicate transactions.
- [ ] Step 4: Reconcile the operating and reserve bank accounts without forced balancing entries.
- [ ] Step 5: Reconcile AR, AP and inventory control totals to their supporting schedules.
- [ ] Step 6: Complete the GST coding scan and GST control-account reconciliation.
- [ ] Step 7: Run P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, AR/AP Ageing and GST reports using controlled parameters.
- [ ] Step 8: Investigate the seeded anomalies listed in the workbook and document each resolution.
- [ ] Step 9: Prepare a one-page management brief covering performance, cash, overdue balances, GST position and top actions.
- [ ] Step 10: Obtain peer reviewer sign-off against the acceptance criteria and preserve the final evidence set.

## Acceptance
- [ ] All five control accounts reconcile
- [ ] Seeded anomalies are found and resolved
- [ ] GST and reporting packs are supported
- [ ] Management brief contains evidence-backed actions
