# Activity 01 checklist

- [ ] Step 1: Open the activity workbook and read the Scenario and Control Matrix sheets before using QuickBooks.
- [ ] Step 2: Sign in to the training QuickBooks Online company supplied by the trainer; confirm that it is a sandbox or training file.
- [ ] Step 3: Open Account and settings and record the current company, sales, expenses and advanced settings in the Evidence Log.
- [ ] Step 4: Enter the legal name, customer-facing name, UEN, GST registration number, contact details and Singapore address from the workbook.
- [ ] Step 5: Set the first month of the financial year to January and confirm the accounting method required by the scenario.
- [ ] Step 6: Confirm SGD as home currency; do not enable multi-currency until the irreversible-setting check is signed off.
- [ ] Step 7: Review GST settings against the 9% standard rate and quarterly filing scenario; do not mark any period as filed.
- [ ] Step 8: Create the proposed user-access matrix without inviting real external users; use trainer-provided dummy addresses only if instructed.
- [ ] Step 9: Capture screenshots of the company, financial-year and GST settings, excluding personal information.
- [ ] Step 10: Complete the Control Checks sheet and record any plan/UI difference in the Exception Log.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Legal identity and financial year match the scenario
- [ ] GST profile is documented
- [ ] Home currency decision is approved
- [ ] Access roles follow least privilege
