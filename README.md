# Quickbooks Accounting System for Small and Medium Enterprises

WSQ courseware for **TGS-2020505113** · Accounting and Tax Systems (ACC-ICT-4001-1.1).

## Learner materials

- `courseware/` — trainer PPTX, learner-slide PDF, Learner Guide and Lesson Plan
- `activities/` — ten progressive QuickBooks SME activities with realistic CSV/XLSX mock data
- `LG-TGS-2020505113-Quickbooks Accounting System for Small and Medium Enterprises-15.0.md` — accessible Markdown mirror of the Learner Guide

## Design boundary

The slide deck is concept-led and visual. Detailed click-by-click procedures, evidence requirements and acceptance criteria live in the Learner Guide and activity packs.

## Scenario

All activities follow Orchid Bay Trading Pte Ltd, a fictional Singapore SME. All names and financial records are mock data for training only.
